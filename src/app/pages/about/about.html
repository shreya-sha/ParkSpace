What this portal is about

A no-brokerage parking space marketplace for city renters

ParkSpace solves a practical city problem: rented flats without parking. Owners with unused parking slots can list them, and customers can discover + book by city and area.

Core MVP capabilities

  • Role-based users: Owner, Customer, Admin
  • Vehicle profile with car-size support
  • Space listings with city/locality and monthly pricing
  • Plan based inquiry visibility (Free/Basic/Premium)
  • Booking lifecycle with active/inactive status

Who uses it

Customer: searches parking, adds vehicle, buys plan, books space.
Owner: posts parking inventory, uploads images, manages bookings.
